## Design Context

### Users
Top tech recruiters and hiring managers (SWE/MLE roles) skimming candidate portfolios — typically 30–90 seconds per visit, often on a laptop between meetings, sometimes on mobile. Secondary: engineers doing peer review before an interview loop.

### Use Cases
- Decide in under a minute whether Justin Kim is worth a phone screen
- Verify claimed skills against real projects (GitHub links, live demos)
- Get a feel for craft/taste — does this person care about quality?

### Brand Personality
Spotify-inspired, music-forward, technically confident. Three words: polished, playful, credible. The site should feel "super cool" (memorable, alive — e.g., live Spotify now-playing integration) while never obscuring the professional signal: experience, projects, skills.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark-first, Spotify-themed UI (greens/dark surfaces), Next.js 15 + Tailwind v4 + Motion/GSAP + R3F for 3D moments. Anti-references: generic Tailwind AI-slop portfolios (purple gradients on navy, identical card grids), template portfolios (Brittany Chiang clones without personality).

### Design Principles
1. Recruiter-first hierarchy: name, role, proof-of-work reachable in one scroll or one click
2. Delight is seasoning, not the meal — animations must never slow down skimming
3. Every claim links to evidence (repo, live demo, or writeup)
4. Dark theme with real contrast discipline, not glow-on-navy defaults
5. Fast on mid-tier hardware; 3D degrades gracefully
